The Hardware

Trophy Case

Freeform awards, earned the hard way. No trophy here is invented — every one of these actually happened, live, in front of everybody.

The Tight End Timeshare
Slot 8

Kyle Pitts in round five. Dallas Goedert in round ten. Juwan Johnson in round eleven. Dalton Schultz in round twelve. Four tight ends, one roster, zero explanation offered or requested.

The Time Machine
Slot 6

Jayden Daniels came off the board in round six — years ahead of when the rest of the room had even opened that folder. Bold. Lonely. Very bold.

Daylight Robbery
Slot 5

Tyrone Tracy was still sitting on the board in round twelve. Slot 5 didn't hesitate, didn't celebrate, just took him and moved on like it happens every day.

Premature Commitment
Slot 2

Round four is early for anything. Round four is very early for Joe Burrow, a proposal the rest of the table wasn't remotely ready to hear.

The Quarterback Stampede
The whole room

Rounds eight and nine: Prescott, Stafford, Herbert, Dart, Lawrence, Mahomes, and Nix all left the board inside twenty picks, like someone rang a bell nobody else heard.

Buzzer Beater
Slot 1

AJ Barner, pick 169 of 180 — proof there was still a usable tight end left on the floor with one round to go, and somebody was paying attention.
